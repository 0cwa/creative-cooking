          Remaining fixture gaps
          1. Two devices of one person plus a fork: Ana's two devices and Ben approve incompatible successors while partitioned, delivered reordered and duplicated. Expect two visible heads, Ana counted once, no cursor-chosen authority, offline edits kept.
          2. Consent replay plus AI: replay one recipe consent for another recipient, or send a second person's inferred allergy to an external prompt. Expect hold or deny; an explicit personal on-device action still allowed.
          3. Durability and side effects: crash between document mutation and outbox commit; deliver a duplicate purchase envelope and two adults' distinct purchases. Expect all-or-nothing on reopen, one event for the duplicate, two events for two purchases.
          4. Run one bounded fixture only after TAlkCo picks its first application surface and observer budget. Still unaddressed: crypto, crash atomicity, context unlinkability, late-reader audience, ratchet restore, host custody.

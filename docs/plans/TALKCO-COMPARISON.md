## Implications in each direction

**For TAlkCo:** Cooking gives a concrete, bounded domain where CRDT collaboration is useful without making it a universal protocol: stable recipe/list identities, append-only purchase events, compensating corrections, and visible same-field loss. Its Loro spike is evidence for narrow convergence and reopen behavior—not authority, crypto, fork truth, transaction atomicity, or human behavior. TAlkCo should add a “domain semantics” fixture case to its shared acceptance corpus and keep permission/trust graphs distinct from content history. Avoid porting adult/guardian governance into channels or community roles.

**For Cooking from TAlkCo:** Keep domain meaning, CRDT merge, adult authority, key release, delivery, and storage separately owned. Treat denial of service and metadata leakage as product tradeoffs.

## Small common fixture interface

A compact JSON case can be shared conceptually: `caseId`, `actors[{personId,deviceIds}]`, `contexts[{id,kind,policyVersion,head,allowedAudience}]`, `objects[{id,kind,scope,parents,semanticEvents}]`, `operations[{id,actorDevice,person,context,object,parents,policyVersion,recipient,consentRef,arrival}]`, `faults[{partition,duplicate,reorder,withhold,crashAt,rollbackTo}]`, and expected per-client `materializedState`, `authorityHeads`, `pending/quarantineReasons`, `disclosures`, `durableOutbox`, and `exportableHistory`. Keep adapter-specific semantics in `kind` codecs; never assume Loro’s state shape is a TalkCo message model.

Three small counterexamples can falsify broad claims cheaply:

1. **Two devices, one person + fork:** Ana’s two devices and Ben each approve incompatible same-parent membership successors while partitioned; deliver them reordered/duplicated. Expected: two visible heads, two Ana devices count once, no cursor-selected authority, offline local edits retained.
2. **Scope/consent replay + AI:** a consent binds one recipe/context/recipient; replay it for another recipient or include a second person’s inferred allergy in an external prompt. Expected: changed recipient/scope held or denied; explicit personal-only local action still allowed despite stricter unrelated group rules.
3. **Durability and domain side effect:** crash between local document mutation and outbox commit; separately deliver duplicate purchase envelope and two distinct adults’ purchase events. Expected: reopen has either full atomic command or none, duplicate transport one event, two physical purchases remain two events, no claim that sync prevents overbuy.

## Is another research round worth it?

**Decisive before Cooking implementation:** (1) final choice/consent for household membership and recovery presets; (2) exact AI data-flow/audience rules, including inferred third-party facts; (3) atomic native/web persistence and failed-commit recovery; (4) late/stale causal history and content rebuild limits; (5) relay retention, backup and operator burden acceptable to target users. These need product decisions or focused implementation evidence, not another broad model round.

**Already answered enough to defer:** Loro can merge the specific tested reordered/duplicate updates; a single LWW-like register does not preserve all concurrent intents; device count is not adult count; threshold recovery cannot silently become membership; relay order is not authority; a ciphertext host cannot promise recall; candidate lists are not an adopted stack. Preserve these bounded statements and move on.

**Deferable/conditional:** Broad cryptographic construction selection, sophisticated deniability, TACo/threshold release, censorship-resistant discovery, distributed custody economics, erasure coding, and TalkCo package adoption. Reopen only if a concrete product promise requires them or the smallest common fixture demonstrates a failure that changes architecture. Licensing is unchecked; verify only if a named package is proposed for reuse.

**Recommendation:** no broad council now. After TAlkCo declares its first application surface and observer budget, run one bounded common fixture. No experiment was run in this comparison. The fixture should report per-client state, authority heads, pending decisions, disclosures, durable outbox and observer-visible identifiers, timing and size. An integrated candidate changes the decision only if it meets the same consent, context separation, recovery and fault outcomes with lower measured maintenance burden. If neither meets the observer budget or recovery requirements on the first required platform, defer that surface rather than select an engine from diagrams.

**Already answered:** Existing-data migration is explicitly excluded from this fresh-data scope. Existing installations must never be silently reset; no migration task is added here. The current model covers distinct-person versus device votes and forks, and content, recipient, purpose, consent and replay decisions. Those are model results, not real cryptographic verification. Atomic crash transactions remain unproved. TAlkCo context unlinkability, late-reader audience, ratchet restore and host custody remain unresolved and no experiment here addresses them.

The fixture's person mapping must be hidden inside its harness: TAlkCo sphere aliases must not become wire identifiers or a global correlation ID. Cooking adult IDs exist within a household; they are not equivalent to cross-context identities. Signed content audit and provenance can conflict with TAlkCo deniability, so do not port a wire format without testing its threat model. Silence is not consent. A personal recovery package cannot weaken a shared grant or automatically confer a household vote. Communal privacy governs release and cooperative behavior, not DRM: authorized recipients can copy plaintext, and free text can contain inferences no classifier reliably detects.

Cooking offers TAlkCo compact real-Loro and domain counterexamples, independently composable policy boundaries and a concrete household rogue-majority recovery problem. TAlkCo offers Cooking the distinctions between scope access and unlinkability, key release and custody, and delivery and authority. Neither project has evidence for directly reusing the other's production engine; share fixture vocabulary first.
